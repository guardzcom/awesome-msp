## Software Bundles and Suites, RMM & PSA
- [Atera](https://www.atera.com/) - All-in-one platform combining remote monitoring and management with professional services automation.
- [Barracuda MSP](https://www.barracuda.com/products/managed-xdr/msp) - MSP program covering managed XDR, email protection, backup, and network security.
- [ConnectWise](https://www.connectwise.com/) - PSA, RMM, remote access, documentation, and security products on the Asio platform.
- [Gorelo](https://gorelo.io/) - Combined RMM, PSA, and documentation platform built for MSPs.
- [Kaseya 365](https://www.kaseya.com/) - Kaseya and Datto suite covering RMM, PSA, backup, security, and documentation, sold as per-endpoint and per-user bundles.
- [ManageEngine Endpoint Central MSP](https://www.manageengine.com/msp/) - Multi-tenant endpoint management with patching, software deployment, and remote control.
- [N-able](https://www.n-able.com/) - RMM, backup, and security platform for MSPs (formerly SolarWinds MSP).
- [NinjaOne](https://www.ninjaone.com/) - Endpoint management platform with RMM, patching, backup, and remote access (formerly NinjaRMM).
- [Pulseway](https://www.pulseway.com/solutions/msp-software) - RMM and PSA platform with mobile-first management, owned by Kaseya since 2025.
- [SuperOps](https://superops.com/) - Unified PSA and RMM platform for MSPs.
- [Syncro](https://syncrosecure.com/) - Combined PSA, RMM, and remote access platform (now Syncro XMM).

## Remote Support, RMM & Device Monitoring
- [BeyondTrust Remote Support](https://www.beyondtrust.com/products/remote-support) - Remote access and support platform with session auditing (formerly Bomgar).
- [ConnectWise ScreenConnect](https://www.screenconnect.com/) - Remote support and access tool (formerly ConnectWise Control).
- [Level](https://level.io/) - Flexible, intuitive, and secure remote monitoring and management (RMM) platform.
- [LogMeIn Rescue](https://www.logmein.com/products/rescue) - Remote support platform with remote access, file transfer, and diagnostics, from GoTo.
- [Splashtop](https://www.splashtop.com/) - Remote access and remote support software.
- [TeamViewer](https://www.teamviewer.com/) - Remote access, control, and support across devices and platforms.

## PSA, Service Desk & CX
- [Autotask PSA](https://www.datto.com/products/autotask-psa) - Cloud PSA with ITIL-aligned ticketing, open APIs, and integrations with Datto RMM.
- [ConnectWise PSA](https://www.connectwise.com/platform/psa) - PSA with help desk, sales, procurement, time tracking, and billing (formerly ConnectWise Manage).
- [DeskDirector](https://www.deskdirector.com/) - Client portal for MSPs that streamlines ticket submission, approvals, and client communication.
- [HaloPSA](https://www.usehalo.com/managed-services) - All-in-one PSA software for managed service providers.
- [Invarosoft](https://www.invarosoft.com/) - Client experience platform combining vCIO, CSAT, warranty tracking, and engineer scheduling.
- [Kaseya BMS](https://www.kaseya.com/products/bms/) - Kaseya's PSA and business management solution.
- [Rev.io](https://www.rev.io/) - PSA and billing platform for MSPs (formerly Tigerpaw).
- [Thread](https://www.getthread.com/) - Service desk that meets clients in Slack, Teams, mobile, or desktop chat.
- [TimeZest](https://www.timezest.com/) - Appointment scheduling for MSP technicians, integrated with the PSA.

## BDR
- [Acronis](https://www.acronis.com/) - Backup, disaster recovery, and data protection for MSPs.
- [Arcserve ShadowProtect](https://www.arcserve.com/) - Image-based backup and disaster recovery (formerly StorageCraft).
- [Barracuda Backup](https://www.barracuda.com/products/backup) - Backup with offsite replication and cloud-to-cloud protection for Microsoft 365.
- [Cove Data Protection](https://www.n-able.com/products/cove-data-protection) - Cloud-first backup for servers, workstations, and Microsoft 365, from N-able.
- [Datto BCDR](https://www.datto.com/products/siris/) - Appliance-based business continuity with ransomware detection and verified restores (SIRIS), now part of Kaseya.
- [Dropsuite](https://dropsuite.com/) - Cloud-to-cloud backup for Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace, now owned by NinjaOne.
- [Hornetsecurity VM Backup](https://www.hornetsecurity.com/en/services/vm-backup/) - Backup and replication for Hyper-V and VMware (formerly Altaro VM Backup).
- [Slide](https://slide.tech/) - BCDR appliance and cloud built for MSPs, with image-based backup and fast virtualization.
- [Unitrends](https://www.unitrends.com/products/enterprise-backup-software) - Backup and recovery across virtual, physical, and cloud environments, now part of Kaseya.
- [Veeam](https://www.veeam.com/) - Backup and replication with instant VM recovery and Microsoft 365 backup.

## Email Security
- [Barracuda Email Protection](https://www.barracuda.com/products/email-protection) - Email security covering spam, phishing, account takeover, and backup.
- [Check Point Harmony Email & Collaboration](https://www.checkpoint.com/harmony/email-security/) - API-based protection for Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace email and collaboration apps (formerly Avanan).
- [INKY](https://www.inky.com/) - Behavioral email security with in-message warning banners for users.
- [Mesh](https://www.meshsecurity.io/) - Email security platform built for MSPs, with multi-tenant management, owned by Bitdefender since 2025.
- [Mimecast](https://www.mimecast.com/) - Email security, archiving, and continuity.
- [N-able Mail Assure](https://www.n-able.com/products/mail-assure) - Cloud email filtering and archiving for MSPs.
- [OpenText Core Email Threat Protection](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/email-security/email-threat-protection/) - Cloud email filtering and threat protection (formerly Zix and AppRiver).
- [Proofpoint](https://www.proofpoint.com/us/solutions/combat-email-and-cloud-threats) - Email and cloud threat protection, with Proofpoint Essentials for SMBs.
- [SpamTitan](https://www.spamtitan.com/) - Email filtering with antivirus and malicious-URL detection, from CyberSentriq (formerly TitanHQ).

## Endpoint Protection and EDR
- [Bitdefender GravityZone](https://www.bitdefender.com/business/enterprise-products/endpoint-detection-response.html) - Endpoint protection, EDR, and MDR with a multi-tenant console for MSPs.
- [CrowdStrike Falcon](https://www.crowdstrike.com/endpoint-security-products/falcon-platform/) - Cloud-native endpoint protection and EDR platform.
- [Microsoft Defender for Business](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/business/endpoint-security/microsoft-defender-business) - Endpoint protection and EDR for small and medium businesses, included in Microsoft 365 Business Premium.
- [SentinelOne](https://www.sentinelone.com/) - Autonomous endpoint protection and EDR on the Singularity platform.
- [Sophos Endpoint](https://www.sophos.com/en-us/products/endpoint-antivirus) - Endpoint protection with anti-ransomware, exploit prevention, and EDR (formerly Intercept X).
- [ThreatLocker](https://www.threatlocker.com/) - Application allowlisting, ringfencing, and elevation control.

- [Field Effect](https://fieldeffect.com/) - MDR platform covering endpoints, networks, and cloud services, sold through MSPs.
- [Guardz](https://guardz.com/) - Unified security platform built for MSPs, covering identity (ITDR), email, endpoint, browser, cloud data, and security awareness training, with 24/7 MDR.
- [Huntress](https://www.huntress.com/) - Managed EDR, ITDR, and SIEM backed by a 24/7 SOC, built for MSPs.
- [Sophos MDR](https://www.sophos.com/en-us/products/managed-detection-and-response) - Managed detection and response service from Sophos.
